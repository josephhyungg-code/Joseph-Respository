Movement to note: The Line @ Tanjong Rhu's $2,054 psf ask is a live read on how freehold stock right by a brand-new station is pricing today, and it's under-running the project's recent average.
- **D19 (Kovan/Serangoon) — pure investment numbers:** Freehold is genuinely scarce here — the large majority of D19 condo stock is 99-year leasehold — so the handful of FH projects (Bliss @ Kovan, Urban Residences, The Gazania/Lilium near Bartley) trade at a structural PSF discount to D15: Bliss @ Kovan's $1,246 psf vs. D15 freehold routinely clearing $2,000+ psf is the gap in one number. Add the Cross Island Line (Hougang interchange, targeted ~2030, with planners flagging Serangoon North/Defu/Hougang as "next hot areas") plus the rarity premium freehold commands in a leasehold-dominated district, and the numbers case is: scarcity + a coming rail catalyst + a price gap that has room to close.
