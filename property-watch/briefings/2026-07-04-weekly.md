## Watch notes — threshold crossings

**Hard filters (reminder):** D15/D19 · Freehold or 999-yr · **> 1,000 sqft** ·
S$2.0M–2.2M · **PSF < S$1,700** · built 2000+ · **≥ 3 bedrooms**.

- **PSF ceiling (S$1,700):** *nothing tracked is near it from below.* Both
  tracked projects sit **over** the ceiling — Carpmael ~S$1,811, Jansen ~S$2,050.
  The gap to the ceiling is the wrong sign (they'd need to fall ~6–20% on PSF).
- **Price band (S$2.0–2.2M):** Jansen House **does** print inside the band
  (e.g. Nov-2025 ~S$2.03M, Dec-2025 ~S$2.11M, Jan-2026 ~S$2.06M), and Carpmael's
  Dec-2025 sale at S$2.30M is just above it — but **every one of these busts the
  PSF ceiling**, because at ~S$2,000+ psf a S$2.0–2.2M ticket buys a ~1,000–1,100
  sqft unit, not the >1,000 sqft *value* the filter is trying to find. The band
  and the PSF ceiling are in tension for these two projects.
- **Bedroom filter:** Carpmael's 3-BR stock is 1,076–1,271 sqft (clears size and
  bedroom); Jansen's in-band units are compact (~1,012 sqft) — right on the
  1,000 sqft line. Verify exact strata area before treating any as a pass.

**Bottom line for the watch list:** on transacted data, **no tracked project
currently clears the PSF < S$1,700 rule.** The trigger to watch for is a D15/D19
freehold/999-yr **≥3-BR, >1,000 sqft** caveat printing **below S$1,700 psf**
inside the S$2.0–2.2M band — none observed this run.
