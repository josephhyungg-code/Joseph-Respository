- **D19 freehold** sits **under** the cap: Oasis Garden ~$1,673, Fiorenza ~$1,504. At 1,216 sqft the cap is ~$1,809, so Oasis Garden leaves **~$135 psf (~8%)** of headroom — the budget genuinely stretches to 3BR size *only in D19*.
- **D19 Kovan leasehold** is cheaper still ($1,458–1,908 psf) but disqualified on tenure.

**Read:** the market gap is favourable only in D19 freehold. In D15 you buy freehold-in-budget by giving up size; in D19 you keep size and stay under the cap — provided you accept Bartley/MacPherson-fringe over prime Katong.

## Filter trade-off — new implication
The old framing was **세대수 vs PSF vs 지역** (unit-count vs PSF vs district). This week the intersection has collapsed to a cleaner statement: **every tracked miss fails on exactly one filter, so the decision is "which single filter to relax."**

- **Keep everything hard** → the buyable set is **Oasis Garden + Carpmael Thirty-Eight**. Small, but real.
- **Relax tenure (freehold → allow leasehold)** → the **Kovan trio** (Regency 1,055 sqft from $1.58M, Residences up to 1,442 sqft, Midtown) opens the best *size-for-quantum* on the board.
- **Relax the new-launch rule (999-yr new build OK)** → **Jansen House** (999-yr, 1,012 sqft, ~$2.04M) opens, and Kovan Jewel/Straits come into view.

So the trade-off is no longer three-way. It's a single lever: **tenure buys you size; the new-launch rule buys you 999-yr fresh stock; holding both hard costs you breadth but keeps you to genuinely seasoned freehold.** Given Joseph's freehold priority, the recommendation is to keep both hard and treat Oasis Garden's 1,216 sqft stack + Carpmael's $2.2M unit as the live shortlist, with Kovan Regency held as the explicit "if we ever flex tenure" fallback.
