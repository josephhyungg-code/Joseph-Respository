## PSF cap vs market gap
For a ≥1,000 sqft unit in the $2.0–2.2M band, the **effective PSF ceiling is ~$2,000–2,200 psf at 1,000 sqft**, tightening to **~$1,818–2,000 psf at 1,100 sqft** and **~$1,540–1,700 psf at 1,300 sqft**. Against that cap:
- **Above the cap (gap $200–400 psf):** all post-2020 freehold — The Lilium ~$2,321, The Gazania ~$2,618, Kovan Jewel ~$2,140 — plus the newly-pricey The Line @ Tanjong Rhu ~$2,346 and Bliss @ Kovan's current 3BR asks ~$2,408.
- **At / below the cap:** every pass and lead — The Quinn $1,940, Carpmael $2,044, Oasis Garden $1,673, Vacanza $1,632, Paya Lebar Res. $1,831, Fiorenza $1,504.
- **Takeaway:** the budget clears the market **only for 2012–2020-vintage freehold in non-prime pockets**. Anything newer or more central is structurally $200–400 psf over the cap.

## Filter trade-offs (units vs PSF vs region) — new implication
The binding constraint has **flipped since June**. In June the story was simply "freehold ≥1,000 sqft in-band is rare." With the wider universe it's sharper: **the $2.0M floor is now filtering as hard as the $2.2M ceiling.**
- **Units:** the passes come from mid/boutique projects (The Quinn 139u, Carpmael 16u), yet the *ideal-size* 3BR stacks live in the large developments (Vacanza 473u, Flamingo 393u) — with better liquidity but in-band economics that don't line up (Vacanza below floor, Flamingo above ceiling).
- **PSF vs region:** D19 non-prime freehold gives the lowest PSF (The Quinn $1,940, Oasis $1,673) at the cost of location; D15 East Coast commands $2,044–2,346 psf for the MRT/liveability premium; D14 sits between but its good 3BR are 1,260–1,313 sqft, pushing total price over the ceiling.
- **The sweet spot** is a **~1,050–1,150 sqft 3BR** (small enough to stay under $2.2M, large enough to clear 1,000 sqft) in **2012–2020 freehold** — precisely the two passes. Two levers would each roughly double the qualifying set: widening the size floor toward ~1,000–1,080 sqft, or flexing the price floor down ~$300k to reach Vacanza's value stack.
