## Filter trade-offs — what this week adds

1. **Freehold is the binding constraint in Bartley/Woodleigh.** That whole modern, MRT-linked belt is 99-yr leasehold. Holding the freehold line there deletes every large, new, transit-connected option — you're left with boutique FH (The Quinn) or nothing. This is the district where the tenure filter costs the most.
2. **Unit-count and freehold pull in opposite directions.** The "hidden 3BR stack in a large development" thesis mostly fails in scope: large projects here are leasehold, and the large freehold ones (Amber belt, Blossoms) are above cap. **Large-project × freehold × in-band is close to an empty set.** Palm Grove (999-yr, 111u) is the exception that proves the rule.
3. **The cap now sits below where most freehold 3BR trades.** ≤$2,200 psf is under the current market for FH 3BR in most of D15 and the firmer parts of D19/D14. The band survives on lower-PSF pockets — which means **size flex up is the natural pressure valve** (1,300–1,500 sqft lowers the required PSF and is exactly what makes Palm Grove and Jade viable).
4. **A tracked project is firming out of band.** Tangerine Grove's jump from ~$1,404 to ~$1,862 psf is this week's clearest movement signal: D19 Paya-Lebar-side freehold is hardening, and the cap will bite harder over time.
5. **Ranked relaxations by leverage:** **(a) tenure → 99-yr** unlocks the entire Bartley/Woodleigh large-project belt at similar/lower PSF; **(b) size up → 1,300–1,500 sqft** lowers required PSF and is already doing the work for the top picks; **(c) price floor down → ~$1.8–2.0M** would capture The Quinn's standard 3BR and Fiorenza's stale-looking listing.
