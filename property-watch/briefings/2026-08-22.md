## PSF cap vs market gap

The **$2,100 psf cap is not the binding constraint.** Ranked by how far *under* the cap the in-band freehold candidates trade:

Siglap V ~$1,500 · Le Crescendo ~$1,700 · Oasis Garden $1,673 · Elliot $1,690–1,806 · Flamingo $1,910 · The Tembusu $1,940 · Carpmael $2,045 · Bliss penthouses $1,246–1,281.

Because these all clear the cap, the thing that actually blocks a purchase is the **$2.2M quantum ceiling**: to be *simultaneously* under-cap, ≥1,000 sqft, and ≤$2.2M, you're forced into a **~1,000–1,076 sqft 3BR** (Carpmael, Tembusu, Le Crescendo entry) or a **large-plate 2BR penthouse** (Bliss). The one place the cap *does* bind is **core D15** — Katong/Amber/Tanjong Rhu freehold trades **$2,300–2,830 psf**, above the cap, so that belt is out on both PSF and quantum; only the Frankel/Bedok/Siglap fringe stays under-cap in D15.

## Filter trade-off — new implication (세대수 vs PSF vs 지역)

**The trade-off has shifted from "D15 vs D19" to "quantum ceiling vs floor-plate size."**

- **세대수 (development size):** In-band *large* freehold developments exist only on the D14/D19 fringe (Le Crescendo 228u, Vacanza 473u, Tembusu 337u), and only their **entry** 3BR stack fits. The large D15 freehold estates are all above cap. The clean passes in preferred areas are **boutique** (Carpmael 16u, Fiorenza 28u) with **near-zero liquidity** — a pass today can vanish with no backup unit. More units ⇒ more shots at a qualifying unit, but that inventory sits in the less-preferred districts.
- **PSF:** Sub-cap PSF is available almost everywhere except core D15 — but you **cannot** have a large freehold floor plate *and* sub-cap PSF *and* ≤$2.2M at the same time. Something has to give.
- **지역 (region):** The most-preferred area (D15 core) is exactly where freehold is priced out. In-band freehold clusters in **D14 Paya Lebar** (Le Crescendo) and **D19 Kovan/Woodleigh** (Tembusu, Bliss, Oasis) — the investment-numbers districts. Bartley/Woodleigh/MacPherson specifically is mostly leasehold, so the newly added D19 sub-areas yield little freehold.

**Highest-leverage single flex:** raising the quantum ceiling by **~$150k ($2.2M → ~$2.35M)** converts three freehold near-misses — **Elliot 1,313 sqft ($2.35M)**, **Oasis Garden 1,216 sqft ($2.28M)**, **Tangerine Grove 1,184 sqft ($2.30M)** — into genuine **large** 3BR passes, two of them in preferred districts. That single move buys more, and larger, freehold 3BRs than relaxing the PSF cap or the tenure rule would.
