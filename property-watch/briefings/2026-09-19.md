## Filter trade-offs — new implications this cycle

- **Tenure is the binding constraint in Bartley/Woodleigh/MacPherson.** That pocket is leasehold-dominated; hold freehold and supply there is ~zero. Every qualifying D19 unit is in **Kovan/Hougang**, not Bartley/Woodleigh. If the buyer truly wants Bartley/Woodleigh, relaxing to 99-yr opens **Kovan Regency's in-band 1,055 sqft 3BR** immediately.
- **The ≥1,000 sqft rule is what closes D14 Eunos.** Urban Treasures (Eunos, FH) has in-band 3BR at ~S$1.95–2.0M — but 883 sqft. Drop the size floor to ~880 sqft and D14 Eunos opens at once.
- **The trilemma is explicit: freehold + large + central — pick two.** Staying in-band with a ≥1,000 sqft 3BR pushes you *out* of prime D15 and *into* either D19 Kovan/Hougang freehold (cheaper psf, further out) or the D15 East-Coast fringe.
- **Hidden low-floor stacks are the only way into central freehold under the cap.** One Amber / Flamingo Valley / Vacanza clear the band solely at the low-floor end — worth standing alerts rather than one-off searches.
